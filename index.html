Common questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does Blindside need an internet connection?

No. This version plays against built-in opponents and works offline.

Are there purchases or ads?

No. Blindside has no in-app purchases and no ads.
