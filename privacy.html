Privacy Policy

Blindside: Siege Strategy · Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

Blindside does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress (your layouts, rating, unlocked pieces and settings) in the app's local storage on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or contact details.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds online features (such as playing against other players), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact support.
